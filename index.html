Our Story

A chef's memoir of spice, fire, memory, and modern Pune glamour.

Inspired by royal Indian feasts and the cosmopolitan energy of Maharashtra, Chef Aarav Kamat envisioned a dining room where nostalgia meets precision—where saffron smoke, cultured butter, kokum acidity, and charcoal perfume tell one seamless story.

Every course at The Saffron Table reflects artisanal produce, progressive plating, and deeply personal hospitality. The result is a restaurant designed for proposals, anniversaries, executive dinners, and travelers seeking Pune's most elevated table.
